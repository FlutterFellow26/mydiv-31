Fairy Tale: Snow White

Snow White is a kind princess who lives with her wicked stepmother.The Queen’s magic mirror says Snow White is the fairest of all. Jealous, the Queen orders a huntsman to kill her in the forest. The kind huntsman lets her escape instead.Snow White runs deep into the woods and finds a tiny cottage where seven friendly dwarfs live. She stays with them and helps with chores.Later, the evil Queen discovers she is alive. Disguised as an old woman, she tricks Snow White into eating a poisoned apple. Snow White falls into a deep sleep.A prince finds her, wakes her with a loving kiss, and they live happily ever after.

Author: Brothers Grimm
